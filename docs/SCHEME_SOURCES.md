Scheme sources

This file records where each scheme's rules and details in backend/schemes.json come from, so they can be checked and updated.

How to read the dates: "Source checked" is the date someone checked the source page or document. It is not the date the rules were issued. Rules can change, so always confirm on the official portal.

Rule for changes: never change a rule or a detail without reading the official source first, and update the last_verified date in schemes.json after checking.

PM-USP CSSS (Education)
Source checked: 2026-11-11
Official source: https://scholarships.gov.in/public/schemeGuidelines/FAQ_DOHE_CSSS.pdf
Where to apply: https://scholarships.gov.in/ (National Scholarship Portal)
Used for: eligibility rules, benefit amounts, documents and application steps.

Rules configured

Class 12 percentile above the 80th percentile for the relevant board and stream
Currently pursuing a degree course
Regular mode course, not a diploma
Institution meets the recognition requirement
Annual family income up to ₹4.5 lakh
Not receiving another scholarship or fee reimbursement

Open items

The guidelines say income "up to ₹4.5 lakh" and the FAQ says "less than ₹4.5 lakh". The guidelines wording is used.
Not yet checked by the app: fresh applicants must have passed Class 12 in the last academic year and be admitted to the first year of a regular course.
PMEGP (Employment and enterprise)
Source checked: 2026-11-12
Official source: https://common-pmegp.msme.gov.in/
Guideline document used: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal/dashboard/notification/Revised_PMEGP_Scheme_Guidelines_07122023_compressed.pdf (revised December 2023)
Where to apply: https://common-pmegp.msme.gov.in/
Used for: benefits, documents and application steps (information only).

Eligibility is not assessed. The guideline document covers 2021-22 to 2025-26. A news report of a Rajya Sabha reply said the scheme was in continuation only up to 31 March 2026, with continuation for the next five years awaiting approval. The current status must be confirmed on the official portal before any eligibility rule is added.

Open items

The subsidy rate table in the guideline PDF could not be read cleanly. The rates in the app (general: 15% urban, 25% rural; special categories: 25% urban, 35% rural) must be confirmed against clause 3.2 of the PDF.
Confirm whether the scheme has been formally continued, and under which guideline.
PM-DAKSH (Skill development)
Source checked: 2026-11-11
Official source: https://socialjustice.gov.in/public/ckeditor/upload/44071717524542.pdf (Scheme Guidelines 2024-25, dated 1 June 2024)
Ministry scheme page (lists corrigenda): https://socialjustice.gov.in/schemes/100
Where to apply: https://pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in/ (registration is on the Skill India Digital Hub)
Other source used for details: PM-DAKSH page on myScheme (https://myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-daksh)
Used for: eligibility rules, benefits, documents and application steps.

Rules configured (these match clause 4.1 of the 2024-25 guidelines)

Category is one of the listed groups: SC, OBC, EWS, DNT, Safai Mitra (including waste pickers)
Age between 18 and 45 years
Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-linked bank account, or an official exemption
Requirements for the chosen job role are met
Annual family income below ₹3 lakh, for OBC and EWS applicants only (no income limit for SC, DNT and Safai Mitras)

Open items

Indian nationality is a condition in the guidelines, but the form does not ask for it.
The ministry page lists a corrigendum dated January 2025. It has not been checked, so confirm that it does not change the rules above.
The guidelines do not list the documents to upload. The document list in the app is general, so confirm the exact documents on the portal.
The registration steps follow the guidelines (Skill India Digital Hub, OTP verification). Confirm the current process on the portal.
The PM-DAKSH portal was slow or unreachable at times, so the ministry's guideline PDF is used as the official source link.
PM-SYM (Pension and social security)
Source checked: 2026-11-06
Official source: https://maandhan.in/

Rules configured

Unorganised worker
Entry age between 18 and 40 years
Monthly income up to ₹15,000
Not covered by EPFO, ESIC or NPS
Not an income-tax payer
